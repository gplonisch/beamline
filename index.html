Beamline

Route light from the lamps to the targets. Each target wants one exact colour, and you have a fixed number of pieces. Red and green arriving together make yellow.

Controls

Keyboard: arrow keys to move, Enter to cycle a cell, R to clear, N and P to change level.

Levels

Pieces

/Mirror. Turns a beam ninety degrees.
\The other mirror, turning the other way.
SSplitter. Passes light on and sends a copy clockwise.

Click or press Enter on an empty cell to cycle through the three, then once more to clear it.
